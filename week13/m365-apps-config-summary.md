Channel Recommendation:



Monthly Enterprise Channel is the best fit for ACME. It delivers foreseeable updates and supported changes on a scheduled monthly evaluation. The stability of the channel delivers access to new features with stability and testing time, without the older semi-annual cadence.



Management in Intune:



Once set in Intune, devices pull updates from the Office Content Delivery Network and using background update policies and Delivery Optimization. When deploying, the Configuration designer and assigned as required, Intune reevaluates and enforces the selected channel on policy refresh—so conflicting channel policies can switch device channels. 



App Configuration and Deployment:



App configuration and policies apply separately from the deployment policy that installs the M365 Apps suite.
