The profile currently enforces a Time-out policy and it addresses the issue of company machines being left on and logged in. This will target the machines that are used for onboarding processes and minor company business work, so multiple people can used the machines for multiple different uses. All they would need is valid log in credentials.

These policy changes for some of the devices have been verified to be fuctional and can be seen by going to Intune then head to Devices, the device of choice then Device configuration. If there were any misconfigured machines that stay on or have a specific log in still active, re-evaluate the status of the specific device on Intune and we will forward a ticket to the technicians.
