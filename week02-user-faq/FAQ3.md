## User goal:



Adjust microphone so voice comes through and others can hear.



## Tool path tested:



* Control Panel → Hardware and Sound → Sound → Recording → Microphone(Shure MV5C Home Office USB Microphone) → Levels



Steps:



1. Search and open **Control Panel&*.

2. Select **Hardware and Sound**.

3. Find **Sound**.

4. Find **Recording**.

5. Find the microphone with the corresponding brand and model (Shure MV5C Home Office USB Microphone).

6. Find **Levels**

7. Adjust slider and unmute the microphone.
