Wave Composition:





Wave 1:



* IT Staff - They are able to manage situations themselves with the knowledge of possible issues that may come up with machines



* Lab Tech - They will be able to troubleshoot issues themselves and have a idea of what issues that may appear



Wave 2:



* Base level Employee - Some employee's such as cashiers, suppliers, and stocking, may not know how to solve issues on a machine 



* Newly hired staff - New hires may not know certain problems that arise when using a machine.









Wave 3:



* Supervisors - having schedule information on their givin account, any issue on machine will be addressed 





* Human Recourses - having schedule information, employee lists, and access to readable payroll information. Issues will be addressed on devices for this role as soon as possible





Wave 4:



* Executives - access to all employee information, readable and editable payroll information and private information.



* &#x20;Location Manager - access to employee private information, readable payroll information, and private employee information.
